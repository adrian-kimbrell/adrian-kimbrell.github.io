About Me

I'm a self-taught developer with a CS degree from Eckerd College, combining academic foundations with hands-on learning. I started my career at the United States Geological Survey building data systems, then moved to Raytheon as a Systems Engineer before finding my home in fintech at Kaleidoscope.

I've architected systems that process SEC filings, stock data, and corporate intelligence at scale. One module I built generates over $200K annually in revenue.

What drives me is solving complex problems and shipping clean, maintainable code. I'm a detail-oriented perfectionist who also thinks big-picture. Whether it's prototyping a new feature or optimizing system performance, I focus on building things that work well and last.
